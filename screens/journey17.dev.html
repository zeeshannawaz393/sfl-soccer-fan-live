The Football Arcade

Short football games — Penalty Shootout & Spin the Wheel. Built the safer model: play can cost Coins or a free daily ticket, but winnings land in a separate non-withdrawable Bonus Coins bucket (+ Kit Bag gifts), breaking the cash-out loop. The server commits every outcome before the animation — the ball/wheel only visualises a decided result. Odds are disclosed, RTP is balanced (not the prototype's 333%), and every result separates entry cost, gross prize and net change. Arcade, never casino: no near-miss, no auto-repeat.
