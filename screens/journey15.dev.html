Soccer Fan Live · Journey 15 — Transfer & Loan

The Transfer Window

A Fan moves between clubs — permanently or on loan — through one gate: Fan consent AND both Managers' approval AND live eligibility. A Fan is a member, never an asset: no price tags, no "buy/sell." Consent is a major checkpoint and is version-locked — change any term and the Fan must agree again. Origin → Fan → Destination throughout; destination-club takeover only after the move completes.
