Soccer Fan Live · Journey 7 — Formation Live Rooms

Go Live: Formation Rooms

The defining SFL room — Niki-style live, but generic guest seats become football positions (GK, CB, CM, LW, ST…) on a formation pitch. Host broadcasts on video; Fans join open positions as audio guests. Setup & picker are light; the room is a bright pitch. Single-Host rooms show the gold Coins counter only — the Fan Power bar appears only with two valid sides (watchalong Home/Away or a PK), per J8.
