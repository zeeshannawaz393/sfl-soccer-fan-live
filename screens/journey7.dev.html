Go Live: Formation Rooms

Stadium is the post-login home: a Niki-style 2-column grid of live streamers. Tap a card to enter that room. Go Live still opens formation setup. Inside a room, generic guest seats become football positions (GK, CB, CM, LW, ST…) on a formation pitch. Live HUD keeps title + role + close/plus; compact MATCH TIME sits upper-right (no Coins-sent pill). The Possession bar appears only with two valid sides (watchalong Home/Away or a PK), per J8. Tap a filled seat: viewers open the profile card; host and room managers get the full Niki guest sheet (Information, Set as manager, Remove guest, mute 30 min, kick 3 hours, Cancel).
