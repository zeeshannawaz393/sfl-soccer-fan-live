Transfer Market

Buy, transfer and loan virtual players in the light system. Each Fan’s weekly duty is to sell 2 players they pick (existing list-for-sale flow) and buy 2 system-recommended players whose value matches those listings. Player Value is the app-set price (never a wallet balance), moves on real W/D/L (+30/+10/0), and every permanent transfer runs through escrow. P/L is Unrealized until a sale completes. Coins-only.
