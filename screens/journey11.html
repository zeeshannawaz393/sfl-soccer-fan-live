Fan Value

A non-cash fan-performance metric — it grows on your club's real results (Win +30 · Draw +10 · Loss 0), never goes down, and is not a wallet (no coin symbol, no withdraw/convert/transfer). It activates once you seed 36 Coins into your club. Fan Value shows in lime/club colour; only the 36-Coin seed is gold, because that's the one Coin spend.
